## Prototype observation method

[prototype-observer.mjs](recheck/prototype-observer.mjs) is QA-only, read-only telemetry loaded with an explicitly scoped `NODE_OPTIONS=--import` for the two rejection gateways. It compares every own descriptor on `Object.prototype` with the process's baseline, including value/getter/setter identity and flags. It samples every 20 ms, records regular unchanged samples and changes, and takes a final exit sample. It does not modify the prototype, replace methods, access product internals or change any product file. All test inputs still enter through the public loop API.

Every rejected required input has a succeeding ordinary turn in the same gateway PID as that rejection. Gateway PIDs 1884 and 43200 cover the two execution stages. Their samples and exit observations show no persistent prototype change. This directly checks for state left behind by the reported pollution mechanism; it is not a claim about every possible transient state between samples.
