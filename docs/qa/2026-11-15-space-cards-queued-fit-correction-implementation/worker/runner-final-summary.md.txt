## Production change (`Canvas.tsx`)
- **Fit mechanism:** Space cards no longer uses `fitView`. It sets the view with `getViewportForBounds` and `setViewport`, using the opening padding, the 0.5 zoom floor and the 2 ceiling. Opening fit, manual Fit View and the Controls fit options are unchanged.
- **Same-turn mutation:** the pending fit records the draft, `generation` and `revision`. The effect re-reads the live store and drops the fit on any mismatch with the rendered or live draft, then calls `setViewport` in the same synchronous turn. Nothing is queued, so no frame, timer or promise is involved.
- **Readiness:** the fit waits until every card has a positive finite size, the pane is positive and finite, and `viewportInitialized` is true. It retries through `useStore(paneMeasured)`, `viewportInitialized` and `measured`, with no polling.
- **Mocks:** `Canvas.test.tsx` gets one mock entry (`useStoreApi`). The mocked Space-cards suite gets a reactive fake pane and viewport.
