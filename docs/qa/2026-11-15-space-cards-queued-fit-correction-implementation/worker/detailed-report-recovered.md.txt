## The correction (apps/web/src/editor/Canvas.tsx)

- `FIT_PADDING` and `OPENING_MIN_ZOOM` constants; `FIT_VIEW_OPTIONS`, `INITIAL_FIT_VIEW_OPTIONS`, `fitOnOpen` and `<Controls fitViewOptions>` keep exactly their old values, so the opening fit and manual Fit View are unchanged.
- Module helpers: `isLength` (finite and > 0), `paneMeasured(state)` (the selector, named so the mocks can find it) and `cardBounds(definition, measured)`, the union of the draft's own positions and Canvas's measured sizes, looked up with `Object.hasOwn`, `undefined` when any card has no size or a non-positive/non-finite one.
- Hooks: `useReactFlow()` now supplies `setViewport` and the public `viewportInitialized`; `useStoreApi()` for the live pane size; `useStore(paneMeasured)` as the reactive retry trigger (no polling, no timer, no private state).
- The pending record is `{ definition, generation, revision }`, captured right after `spaceCards()` returns `'applied'`.
- The effect: nothing pending, return. Otherwise first drop the fit for good if the rendered `definition`/`generation` or the store's own live `definition`/`generation`/`revision` differ from the record. Then, if the viewport is not initialized, any card is not measured to a positive finite size or the live pane is not positive and finite, keep waiting (the effect runs again when `measured`, `viewportInitialized` or `paneReady` change). Otherwise clear the record and call `setViewport(getViewportForBounds(bounds, pane.width, pane.height, 0.5, 2, FIT_PADDING))` in the same synchronous turn as those checks. There is no `fitView`, no frame, timer, promise or private fit-state write, and nothing is left to clean up.
- Documented in the code comment: why not `fitView`, why `revision` is in the record (undo+redo returns the same draft object), why `paneReady` is a retry trigger only.

Real Canvas on the installed xyflow 12.12.0 and d3-zoom in jsdom, inside `StrictMode` and `ReactFlowProvider`. Fixture: card and pane sizes, a reporting `ResizeObserver`, a `checkVisibility` stub on the renderer element (a pane xyflow cannot measure yet), and one controlled intervention: `window.requestAnimationFrame` can be held and released (delegating to jsdom's own when not held). **This is a controlled component fixture, not native verification.** Oracle: `flow.getViewport()` and a read-only count of xyflow's transform changes.

4. a pane xyflow has not measured: the fit is kept, lands once at the measured size, and is spent.
5. the same pane wait is dropped by Undo+Redo returning the **same draft object** (the test asserts the identity premise and `revision + 2`).
6. the same wait is dropped by an edit.
7. the same wait is dropped by a load.
8. unmount, with frames held and with a fit still waiting for the pane: no console error, no queued fit.

## Deviations from the accepted design

1. Readiness is stricter, per the root disposition: the design only waited for an unmeasured or zero-size card; this also waits for a positive finite pane and `viewportInitialized`, reactively. The design read the pane only through `useStoreApi`; I added the boolean `useStore(paneMeasured)` as the retry trigger (Canvas does not re-render on every pane resize) and the public `useReactFlow().viewportInitialized`.
2. Real-dependency file: added hidden-pane (readiness, ABA, edit, load) cases beyond the design's four. The design's "positive" case covers nothing automatic; I also added the manual Fit View control after Space. Presses use `fireEvent.click(…, {detail: 1})` rather than the gate test's `press` helper.
3. Mocked suite: a reactive fake pane replaces the design's static stub for `Canvas.space-cards.test.tsx`; `Canvas.test.tsx` uses the design's one-entry stub (shaped `{getState}`).
4. Removed an unreachable empty-draft guard from `cardBounds` (a spaced draft has at least two cards); documented in its comment.
5. `Object.hasOwn` retained although M8 shows `isLength` alone suffices.
