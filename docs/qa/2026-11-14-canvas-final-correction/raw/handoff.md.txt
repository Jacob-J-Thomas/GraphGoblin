## Cause 1: d3 `click.drag` cancels the first Remove after a pan

This implements the root architecture (`ui-207-final-correction-architecture.md`). It replaces the plan's proposal to re-enable on `onMoveEnd` after a mouseup.

- `apps/web/src/editor/viewport-gesture.ts` (new) holds one gate per canvas.
  - States: `ready` → `panning` on `onMove` with a `mousemove` source event → `settling` on the next window mouse release → `ready` after a two-stage zero-delay task barrier.
  - Only the second task enables, and only when no mouse move came since (generation check) and the canvas is still mounted (detach cancels the pending task and advances the generation).
  - A later release restarts the barrier. Wheel, touch, null, dblclick and mousedown sources never change the gate.
  - It does not prevent, stop or replay events, and it never force-enables.
- `Canvas.tsx` creates the gate with `useState` and attaches it with `useLayoutEffect` at mount: one passive capture `mouseup` listener on `window`. `onMove` calls `gesture.move(event)` and then the existing `closeCanvasPopovers()`. The gate is exposed as `editing.viewport`.
- `route-editing.ts` adds `viewport` as a required member, so missing wiring is a type error.
- `OrthogonalEdge.tsx` (RouteHandles) reads the gate with `useSyncExternalStore`.
  - While the gate is not ready, Reset route and Remove connection get `aria-disabled="true"` and `data-gesture="panning|settling"`. They keep their hit target, focus and `nopan`, and the existing `aria-disabled` styling dims them.
  - Their handlers refuse on the same rendered value. Remove still stops propagation.
  - On a protected Chat edge, Remove keeps its native `disabled` and `VOICE_STEP_MESSAGE` title and gets no gate attributes.
  - Segment handles are unchanged.

## Tests

**`viewport-gesture.test.ts` (new, 6 tests)**
- Only a mouse move arms the gate, with one notification per state change.
- The gate's first task is queued before a d3-like cleanup. The stand-in records "d3 mouseup after 1 queued", the cleanup runs while the gate is `settling`, and only a later task makes it ready.
- A newer move invalidates the pending barrier in either phase.

**`viewport-gesture.contract.test.tsx` (new, 5 tests)**

These run the real `Canvas` on the installed @xyflow/react 12.12.0, @xyflow/system 0.0.83, d3-zoom 3.0.0 and d3-drag 3.0.0 in jsdom, with real timers and no act environment. Nothing is mocked or patched. The fixture supplies only card and pane sizes and a ResizeObserver that reports them.

Readings are passive: window capture and bubble listeners, target-arrival listeners, and d3-selection's own `__on` record on `jsdom.window`.

1. **Left-button pan:**
   - A capture listener added after mount sees the release with `mouseup.zoom` still registered and no `click.drag`. A listener added after the press never runs.
   - The pan's own click is cancelled and never reaches its target.
   - An immediate Remove click finds Remove showing `aria-disabled` with `settling` and `click.drag` present; the click is cancelled before reaching the button and `e1` stays.
   - A task queued after the release sees `click.drag` gone and the gate still `settling`. A click then reaches the button and is refused, and `e1` stays.
   - At the first moment Remove shows as available (MutationObserver), `click.drag` is absent. The click is delivered and removes `e1` exactly once, as one undo step; Undo restores the definition.
2. **Middle-button pan:** the same settling.
3. **Newer pan:** a second press and move before the first barrier keeps the gate `panning` after both of the first release's tasks. A click is refused, and the second release settles the gate.
4. **Programmatic or wheel end:** a wheel alone never arms the gate. A `setViewport` right after the release replaces xyflow's pending end, so `onMoveEnd` reports `null` and never `mouseup`; the gate still settles and the first click acts.
5. **Unmount mid-settle:** nothing is left behind, and a fresh canvas starts ready.
