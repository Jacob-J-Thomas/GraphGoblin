- Raw-content replay survives republish, filter changes and signing-header changes. Distinct bodies sharing an authored key consume a terminal `deduplicated` receipt and return `DUPLICATE_KEY`; repeating those bytes returns `REPLAYED`. Concurrent, restarted, filtered/pending and historical timestamp-run cases pass. Scope is owner/loop/node across versions.
- Red race cases originally spent cap 1 on an old key after recovery or while its allocated webhook intent remained pending. The reverse ordering—poll commits before an old body claim—is also covered. Transient failure retains reservations; permanent failure releases them and cannot later dispatch that intent.

Credential-bearing response headers and raw transport/process diagnostics stay out of saved trigger evidence.
