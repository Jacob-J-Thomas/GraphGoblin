## 7. Decisions and parameters

- First `node.started` after the node's previous completion identifies a logical visit. Retries
  and restarts keep it; completed/repeated visits, forks and child logs cannot share its state.
- Persist the exact prompt before invoking the adapter. A refused retry re-records the same
  task identity and original resume selection before invocation, clearing a prior init-only
  fresh-session ID. A continuation or repair is a separately identified pending task.
- Durable intent with no proven refusal is uncertain, including a crash before the acceptance
  marker reaches storage. This intentionally favors owner choice over duplicate task submission.
- Preserve specific error causes and non-resumable engine failures. Generic uncertain turns get
  a distinct code; no-session uncertainty also explains the required owner choice in its message.
- Readiness/version/help/auth probes and their directory lifecycle remain WP-F1's design.
  Only actual turn handling uses the new fixed refusal field. Diagnostic matching uses the
  existing 8,192-character ephemeral stderr window and never retains raw bodies in failures.
- No new product timeouts, turn caps, retries, fallback models or session policies. The native
  fixture uses a 60-second observation deadline and a 180-second whole-test deadline, engineering
  test parameters rather than product limits. Existing internal test deadlines stay unchanged.
- An unconfirmed native process outranks refusal text. A synthetic transport regression verifies
  that missing-session stderr cannot conceal `HARNESS_TERMINATION_UNCONFIRMED`.
