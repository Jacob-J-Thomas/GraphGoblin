Its timed process-identity answers are fixture-only, and replay sends managed drain only after validating the response fingerprint against the disposable configuration. A separate process-handle diagnostic against the intermediate launcher implementation confirmed process exit and pipe EOF are separate. A Win32 descendant inherited only the helper's two output handles and stayed alive for four seconds. The launcher exited 1 after 1,243 ms, reporting the configured 1,000 ms experimental deadline while waiting for output closure. The descendant wrote its completion marker about three seconds later and exited normally. The shipping floor is 15 seconds, and the same Windows handle-list mechanism is exercised by the focused acceptance suite. [Intermediate timing output](qa-intermediate-pipe-holder.json).
