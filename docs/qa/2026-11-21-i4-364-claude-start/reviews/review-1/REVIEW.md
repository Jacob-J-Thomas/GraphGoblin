## Started-process evidence, interruption and pipes

The critical new guard is [process.ts:219](G:/ggq/gg-i4-364-claude-start/GraphGoblin/packages/infrastructure/src/claude/process.ts:219) and [process.ts:232](G:/ggq/gg-i4-364-claude-start/GraphGoblin/packages/infrastructure/src/claude/process.ts:232): a child with an assigned PID cannot acquire spawnCode from a later error. Consequently the harness's existing `spawnCode → definitely-not-submitted` mapping is now restricted to a genuine no-process refusal. A failed kill or later child error cannot make accepted/uncertain work look safe to replay.

After refusal, the runner keeps that refusal authoritative while awaiting metadata; a subsequent close does not race it into a successful result. Abort or the existing deadline calls [process.ts:151](G:/ggq/gg-i4-364-claude-start/GraphGoblin/packages/infrastructure/src/claude/process.ts:151), which settles the no-process result immediately without a cause, a kill or an invented timeout. A late stat completion cannot alter the already-resolved result object. Timers and the abort listener are removed by `finish`. Native stat itself is not cancelled; that is a limit of the read operation, not a claim of interruptible filesystem I/O.

The new stdout/stderr handlers always consume pipe error events, preventing never-started pipes from raising an unhandled error. A pipe fault with no PID is secondary to the refusal and is ignored. A pipe fault with a PID sets outputFailed before calling the existing owned-tree termination path ([process.ts:220](G:/ggq/gg-i4-364-claude-start/GraphGoblin/packages/infrastructure/src/claude/process.ts:220)). It does not synthesize a safe start refusal. Existing close/kill acknowledgement and five-second termination-unconfirmed handling remain authoritative; killTree itself is unchanged.

For turns, [harness.ts:517](G:/ggq/gg-i4-364-claude-start/GraphGoblin/packages/infrastructure/src/claude/harness.ts:517) and [harness.ts:519](G:/ggq/gg-i4-364-claude-start/GraphGoblin/packages/infrastructure/src/claude/harness.ts:519) preserve the required ordering: unconfirmed termination, cancellation and transport cutoffs are checked before stderr turn-refusal interpretation. In particular outputFailed cannot be downgraded by a later auth/session/argument refusal; delivery stays uncertain. [harness.ts:163](G:/ggq/gg-i4-364-claude-start/GraphGoblin/packages/infrastructure/src/claude/harness.ts:163) and [harness.ts:549](G:/ggq/gg-i4-364-claude-start/GraphGoblin/packages/infrastructure/src/claude/harness.ts:549) retain termination-unconfirmed ahead of cancellation and diagnosis. Already observed acceptance remains evidence; the patch does not invent acceptance from metadata.
