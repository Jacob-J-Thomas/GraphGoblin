## What was implemented

The required #208 obligation only: the Space cards offer's two buttons use the **same Canvas-owned viewport gate** that guards Remove connection and Reset route after a mouse pan.

- **`Canvas.tsx`**
  - Conflict resolved by keeping both Space cards imports (`spaceCardsEligible`, `SpaceCardsOffer`) and `createViewportGestureGate`. No markers remain.
  - One wiring line: `<SpaceCardsOffer … viewport={gesture} …>`, the existing `useState(createViewportGestureGate)` gate that `RouteEditing.viewport` already uses. Plus a comment. No second gate, no new window listener, no store change.
- **`SpaceCardsOffer.tsx`**
  - New prop `viewport: Pick<ViewportGestureGate, 'state' | 'subscribe'>`; the offer subscribes with `useSyncExternalStore(viewport.subscribe, viewport.state)`, as `OrthogonalEdge` does. The subscription is in the offer, not Canvas, so a gate change re-renders only the panel and never the 100-card canvas.
  - While the gate is not `ready` (`panning`, then `settling`), **Space cards** and **Dismiss suggestion** render `aria-disabled="true"` and `data-gesture="panning|settling"`. They are not `disabled`, not `pointer-events: none` and not hidden, so they stay attached, hittable and focusable and nothing falls through to the canvas. No timeout, no replay.
  - Each click handler is guarded: it returns, silently (no announcement, no focus move, no store call), unless the **rendered** state is ready **and** `viewport.state()` is ready *now*. This is the existing route controls' "refuse on the same rendered state" plus an action-time read of the same gate.
- **Unchanged:** `spaceCardsEligible` and the store's action-time recheck, one history step and conditional save, exact nongeometry/edges/first anchor, the one deliberate fit, canvas focus, the polite announcements, the `!moving` hiding during a card or route drag (that is not the viewport gate, and I did not touch node-drag behaviour), `space-cards.ts` (SHA-256 `8921ad9e67218fa788bb30e401f02702aa097932509d8413c02592d77e0fafd3`, byte-identical to the required hash and to HEAD), `store.ts`, `viewport-gesture.ts`, route actions.

## What the tests establish (jsdom, component level)

`Canvas.space-cards.test.tsx` (the real Canvas and its real gate; only xyflow's `ReactFlow` is stubbed):
- Both buttons stay the same attached elements, enabled (`not disabled`, no `pointer-events-none` class), take focus, and say `aria-disabled`/`data-gesture` = `panning` then `settling`, from the first mouse `onMove` until the release settles. Wheel, touch and programmatic moves never change that. The offer is not hidden.
- Refused while unavailable by click, Enter and Space: store state object unchanged, no step, no fit, no announcement, and focus stays on the button. After release, the first press spaces **once**: one `space cards` step, measured heights, canvas focus, the Undo announcement, one fit.
- Dismiss is refused the same way, then acts once: no `useEditorStore` change at all (a subscriber is never called), same state object, `revision: 0`, no past or future, canvas focus, announcement.
- Stale eligibility: an offer rendered before the store moved is refused by the gate first (store not asked, nothing announced), and after release is refused by the store's own recheck (same state, canvas focus, the refusal text, no fit).
- One gate: exactly one `mouseup` window listener, passive capture, with the offer rendered; it is removed on unmount.
- Offer returning mid-pan (an Undo of a move while panning) is already shown unavailable.
- Direct offer tests with the real gate: one subscription for any number of changes, none after unmount, current state read on return; and a deaf subscription (rendered ready) is still refused at action time.

`Canvas.space-cards.gate.test.tsx` (real xyflow, d3-zoom, d3-drag, same fixture as `viewport-gesture.contract.test.tsx`, a two-card pile with its connection selected): the offer and the Remove toolbar show the same state at each stage; d3's `click.drag` blocker cancels the click after a pan before it reaches Space cards or Dismiss; the click that arrives after d3's cleanup while the gate still settles is delivered but refused; at the first moment the buttons show available the blocker is gone and the press acts once (Space cards: one step, first node, edges and revision as expected, exact Undo; Dismiss: store never written). A second pan invalidates an earlier release; unmount mid-settle leaves nothing, and a new canvas starts ready.

`EditorPage.space-cards.test.tsx`: after Dismiss, with FakeApi, the same store state object, Undo still unavailable, no PUT and no device-copy change after the autosave delay.
