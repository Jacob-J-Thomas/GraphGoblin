## #562: credential-generation dispatch boundary

**Verified by reading the code:**

1. **Atomic check.** `bindCredential` builds a client whose `fetch` puts the bound credential on
   the exact `Request` (a `WeakMap`) and then calls the app client's `withApiKey` fetch.
   `withApiKey` reads `{ key, generation, rejected }` with a single `getState()`. In the same
   synchronous step it compares the bound credential and throws `CredentialChangedError` before
   it sets the header or calls `fetch`.
   - openapi-fetch 0.17 (`dist/index.mjs` lines 69–123) awaits any `onRequest` middleware before
     it calls `fetch(request)`, so this check comes after every middleware and mutation await.
   - The app client adds no middleware (grep: no `client.use(`). Author item 7 is therefore
     currently inert.
2. **Refusal unwrapping.** openapi-fetch's `onError` (`networkErrors`) wraps the refusal as
   `GraphGoblinApiError.network(cause)`. `sendUnder` unwraps it back to `CredentialChangedError`.
   As a result, a refusal never reaches `isOfflineError`, the status-0 outage paths or the global
   mutation cache as a transport failure.
3. **Every inspector write is covered.** A grep of `runs.(cancel|pause|resume|recoverSession|provideInput|signal|replay|confirmDeletion|previewDeletion)`
   in `apps/web/src` finds only the five changed components. `RunLauncher` (`runs.start`) is
   not an inspector write.
4. **Gate identity and live check.** The gate memo depends on `generation` and `rejected`, and
   `open()` checks `isCurrentCredential`. `sessionGate` inherits the gate's credential. The Runs
   list keeps `OPEN_GATE`, which has no credential, so its behaviour is unchanged.
5. **Outcome suppression.** In every component, `crossed(gate)` is evaluated at settlement.
   Navigation, fork IDs, error text and `onConfirmed` are suppressed under another credential, and
   the run/thread/runs cache refresh is kept. `sameCredential(command.gate, availability)` hides an
   earlier credential's outcome once the gate changes.
6. **Same-key save.** It leaves `generation` unchanged (`accept`), so the gate and the review
   are unchanged.

The required copy, focus and reset behaviour matches the root adjudication. The copy is
parameterised with the real control labels (`Replay...` with three dots, not the design's `…`).

## #563: reflow

Assessment of the author's deviations from the design:

- **Header wraps from 640 px (`sm:flex-wrap`).** Accept. The baseline evidence shows nav links
  reaching 1068 px at 640 and 768 px with 200% text on all 7 routes. A header that kept the design's
  "unchanged at 640 px and up" would fail the adjudicated 640/768 containment gate. The candidate
  holds 1440/100, 1440/200 and 320/100 header boxes at 0 differences on the six non-inspector routes
  (I read `browser-candidate/*/baseline-comparison.json`).
- **Run-header `fit-content(50%)`.** Accept. In the baseline's 1440/200 geometry, the controls
  column starts at x=224 while the h1 ends at x=500.8, so the controls covered the run ID and
  intercepted the double click. The candidate moves the controls to x=720. 1440/100 is unchanged
  (0 differences). See F4 for the intermediate widths that no cell holds.
- **Wider label wrapping (`WRAPPING_LABEL`).** Accept. `cn`/`variants` use plain `clsx`, so
  `h-auto` must win the cascade over `h-9`/`h-8`. The candidate's `uncontained` (control text
  vertically inside its control) check passes at 320/200, which shows that it does for fine
  pointers. The coarse-pointer cascade is by construction only.
- **Inherited `wrap-anywhere` on the selected-event column and around `ExecutionContext`.**
  Accept. `overflow-wrap` is inherited, and the exact-JSON `pre` is `white-space: pre`, so the
  only sideways scroller is unaffected. It does lower min-content sizes inside those subtrees.
  At wide widths that has no effect, and at narrow widths words break only where they would
  otherwise overflow.
- **`py-1.5` below 640 px.** Accept. A 44 px coarse Menu plus 2×6 px gives the same 56 px row.
