## Design (what the source does)

`JsonSchemaForm.tsx` only. `LabelLine` (new) is shared by property fields and the raw JSON editor.

- **Coarse Omit** (`OMIT_BUTTON`): `pointer-coarse:h-11 min-w-11 my-0 rounded-md border-strong bg-surface-raised shadow-ledge`, the
  repo's outline-button look at its coarse 44 px size, text centred. The fine look is the same 20 px ghost button (only `justify-center`
  added, which changes nothing at its content width).
- **Coarse label line of an optional field** (`OMIT_ROW`): `pointer-coarse:items-end gap-3 pb-1.5`. The line is as high as Omit's place
  (44 px), the label and any JSON tag sit at its foot, and the label is 12 px from Omit. The 6 px under the line plus the
  `FieldGroup` 6 px gap put Omit 12 px above the field. The form's 16 px gap puts the line's top ≥ 16 px below the previous field.
- **Held place** (both pointers): while there is no Omit, an `aria-hidden` `invisible` `<span>` drawn with the same classes and text
  stands in for it. It is the same size in any font, takes no hit test, no focus and no accessibility node, so Omit appearing moves
  neither label, tag nor field. Required fields hold nothing. The raw editor holds a place only when its schema accepts no input.

## Not proven / residual risks

1. **Emulated touch only:** CDP touch on desktop Edge, default touch area. No real fingers, Android Chrome, iOS Safari, or browser
   zoom-out (which shrinks CSS-px clearances). The 12 px clearance compares with a measured firing threshold of 6–7.5 px (phase2 at
   20 px; the mutant at 6 px here).
2. **Forced colours:** not re-examined in this phase. Phase2's limitation stands: emulation changes media/computed styles but does not
   paint the OS palette, and no real High Contrast run was done. The coarse Omit's `border-strong` becomes the system button border in
   forced colours, but that was not verified.
3. **e2e density:** the e2e grid (x every 6 px, depth every 2 px) caught the mutant on the JSON field but not on the text field, where
   the driver's 1 px grid found 9 wrong taps. The deterministic ≥ 12 / ≥ 16 clearance assertions are what fail first on any spacing
   regression. The dense evidence is in the driver logs, not CI.
4. **CI timing:** the coarse e2e case takes 22.5 s of the 60 s default locally; CI runners may be slower.
5. **Layout cost:** coarse optional rows are about 26 px taller than phase2 (+187 px on a 7-optional-row form). On fine pointers a long
   optional title wraps about 50 px earlier, because Omit's place is held.
6. No WCAG conformance claim is made beyond the repo's ≥ 44 px coarse convention (the target box is 44 × 44 CSS px with 8 px rounded
   corners).
7. Unchanged and out of scope, as before: Omit still discards the typed text; the raw editor's `{}` placeholder; text cannot send
   `null`.
