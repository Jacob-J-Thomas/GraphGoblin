## Outcome

A run follows the definition and logical visit it started with and remains controllable across interruption.

## Priority

3 in the approved next-intent order.

## Units of work

- #298 **U07: Preserve execution and wait identities** — Runs and all wake/input sources agree on immutable execution and logical visit identity.
- #299 **U08: Drain or interrupt deliberately** — Normal and forced stop are observably distinct, and observation can be cancelled independently.
- #300 **U09: Protect state and admission boundaries** — State protections and admission behavior remain true through all supported paths.

## Intent acceptance

Editing, waiting, waking and stopping preserve the correct execution; forced interruption is explicit and recoverable behavior is truthful.

## Source issue coverage

#140, #224, #225, #226, #227, #233, #247, #257, #272

Conditional investigation/measurement bolts require their evidence before corrective implementation. Do not count a negative investigation as a successful fix.

## Specific owner feedback

- Normal shutdown stops new independent admission but lets the active parent/child work reach its result. After a configurable several-minute interactive grace, ask whether to keep waiting or force interruption. Keep waiting has no hidden kill timer. Without UI, normal stop can wait indefinitely; only explicit force-stop interrupts. Queued independent work remains recoverable.
- Definition pinning was already settled: every in-flight execution retains its original immutable definition for its whole lifetime, including draft runs, waits, resumes and restart. Only newly started executions use later edits.

## Approved review refinements

Pin authored execution definitions and visit-bound waits/timers. During normal shutdown retain input/status/cancel/force access and allow required active child/continuation work to finish; explicit force records truthful interruption without blind replay.
