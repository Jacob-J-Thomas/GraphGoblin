## Outcome and acceptance

Normal and forced stop are observably distinct, and observation can be cancelled independently.

## Touched components

- run manager
- scheduler
- gateway lifecycle
- shutdown UI
- API client

## Bolts

- #224 **[I03/U08/B22] Implement normal drain and explicit force-stop** — Normal stop refuses new independent admissions while allowing active parent/child continuations to finish; queued independent work remains recoverable.
- #319 **[I03/U08/B23] Expose the interactive shutdown choice** — After the configured several-minute grace, explain active work and offer keep waiting or force; keep waiting never starts a hidden kill timer and closing a browser tab is not shutdown.
- #233 **[I03/U08/B24] Honor client wait cancellation and deadlines** — Abort/deadline interrupts an in-flight request or sleep promptly; cancelling observation does not cancel the underlying run unless explicitly requested.

Each nondeferred bolt is the sole implementation record for its scope. Current UoW completion requires their evidence and applicable owner acceptance; parked children do not block current completion. Publication is not dispatch.

## Specific owner feedback

- Normal shutdown stops new independent admission but lets the active parent/child work reach its result. After a configurable several-minute interactive grace, ask whether to keep waiting or force interruption. Keep waiting has no hidden kill timer. Without UI, normal stop can wait indefinitely; only explicit force-stop interrupts. Queued independent work remains recoverable.
