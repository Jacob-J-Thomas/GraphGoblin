## Bounded change

Implement normal drain and explicit force-stop

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Normal stop refuses new independent admissions while allowing active parent/child continuations to finish; queued independent work remains recoverable.
- [ ] No-UI normal stop may wait indefinitely. Explicit force interrupts the active tree, records truthful unfinished status and releases the store after executor cleanup.

- [ ] Stop new independent admission before drain while keeping status, input, cancellation and explicit force reachable. Required child/continuation/timer work of active runs must still make progress; independent queued work remains recoverable.
- [ ] After the configurable several-minute grace, offer keep waiting or explicit force. Waiting has no hidden kill timer. End event streams and close listener/store only after the applicable drain/force sequence.

One active plus queued runs; multi-node/child/timer continuations; force during native/script work; new process can reopen store.

Disposable native process with open SSE inspector, input while draining, a child created during drain, parked required work, queued independent work, wait/force and relaunch. Source hook order is a lead, not a native pass.
