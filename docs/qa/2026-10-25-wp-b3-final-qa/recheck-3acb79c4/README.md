**How the scenarios are driven.** A driver spawns the confirmed reset and polls every 5 ms. A reset makes two private copies of the store: the read-only preview's and then the one read under the lock. The driver acts at one of two moments:

- when `graphgoblin.lock` appears, which is after the preview read and before the locked read; or
- when the second copy appears and its marker has recorded the SQLite worker.

**What "unchanged" means.** It compares every record of the base folder before and after the reset: content, size, mtime and file index. Two records are left out: the data directory's own mtime and the lock file record, both of which move because the reset creates and releases its lock. The lock's absence afterwards is checked separately. When the scenario itself changes the tree, the comparison starts from the moment just after that change.
