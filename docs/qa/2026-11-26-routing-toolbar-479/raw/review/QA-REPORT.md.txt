## What was not established (limits)

- **No physical touch hardware or real phone.** The coarse pointer is Chromium emulation with CDP taps, and all runs are headless Edge on Windows. Linux CI Edge was not run.
- **Mouse-pan gating** was not separately probed with a click during settling. That is covered by the rerun `edge-routes.spec.ts`; my drivers wait for `data-gesture` to clear.
- **Store-level stale selection** (a selection left over from before a load, acting before re-render) was not driven in the browser. It is covered by unit tests. Only the conflict-and-reload path (case 14) was exercised, and in light theme only. Sweeps 17–21 also ran in light only, except 18's diagnostic, which ran in dark.
- **Native-baseline equality for coarse, 390 and 320** comes from my rerun of the author's spec in the test-server environment. My exploratory runs on the real API have a taller header at those widths, so they are checked by relationships only.
- **Pre-#479 behaviour** in the new F5 geometries (320×568 and 320×640 coarse) was reasoned, not run, because no base build was used.
- **Forced colours** was not checked beyond the rerun `edge-routes` forced-colours case. Contrast was computed for Remove at Below placement against the canvas and a card only.

## Verdict

PASS WITH LIMITS. Every acceptance item in the issue and the design holds in real Edge:

- the reproduced cases are wholly hittable inside the pane (63/63, full 44 px coarse);
- Remove, Undo and Reset each save exactly once, and readbacks are byte-equal;
- camera moves never write;
- ports and segment handles are not intercepted at the reproduced Fits;
- keyboard and focus work, contrast passes, and the #481 note and the protected and stale refusals all behave.

Gaps found (all P3, see REVIEW.md):

- **F2:** Clamp onto Below's place can cover port centres. This is the design's anticipated gap, and it now has native evidence.
- **F5:** On short, narrow, coarse panes the row can sit partly under the canvas's own Controls panel.
- **F6:** A false `data-overflow` at the row's max-width.

Maintained-regression recommendation (not added to the branch): give `expectReachable`'s overflow assertion the same tolerance F6 proposes. Optionally add a coarse ≤ 360 px case that probes for the Controls panel once F5 is decided.
