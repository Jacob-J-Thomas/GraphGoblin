## Open questions and limits

1. **Focus after later Reset presses.** Design assertion 6 says "Focus stays on the edge". The
   first press moves focus to the edge (unchanged #207 behaviour). A later trusted mouse press on
   the held, `aria-disabled` Reset route gives that button focus (the browser's mousedown), as the
   existing unit test already asserts; the hold's handler deliberately does nothing. The design
   also lists Reset's focus behaviour as unchanged, so I kept it and the spec asserts the actual
   behaviour. Independent QA should confirm this reading.
2. **Clamp when Below covers a handle.** Following the design literally, when Below fits but
   covers a handle and below is the roomier side, Clamp lands on the same place as Below and
   reports `data-covers-handle="true"`. No native case shows it; preferring the other side would be
   a design change.
3. **Clamp in the browser.** Not reachable by window size in this layout (minimum canvas 263 px).
   Unit-tested only.
4. **Not exercised by the new browser spec:** protected voice-step and stale-selection refusals,
   and mouse-pan gating beyond waiting for `data-gesture` to clear. Their code paths are unchanged;
   unit tests and the rerun `edge-routes`/`shared-edge-ids` Edge specs cover them.
5. Implementer browser observations do not replace the separate Claude QA (both themes at every
   size, native click/tap/Reset/Undo, keyboard and contrast), current hosted coverage and CI, the
   GPT opposite-family review and the fresh Astra MAX final audit. #585 routing diagnosis is
   separate.
6. The heavy-suite lock was held idle from 04:45Z to about 07:31Z because the session hit its usage
