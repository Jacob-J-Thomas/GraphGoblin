- First statement is now `if (positions.length !== definition.nodes.length) throw new Error(...)`. The message reads `Space cards needs one position for each of the N cards, but got M.`

## Complexity (N nodes, E edges, P total output ports)

- `spaceCardsEligible`: O(N + E), with early exit.
- `spacedPositions`: O(N + E + P) time and space.
  - Ports are listed once per node (`canvasPorts`) into a `Set`.
  - ID resolution is one pass over nodes. Edge-ID counts are one pass over edges. Adjacency is one more pass over edges. Hash lookups are O(1) on average.
  - Entry selection is O(N). Discovery enqueues each card once (mark on enqueue, queue cursor) and scans each counted adjacency once. The leftover scan is O(N). Stacking and the final shift are O(N).
  - There is no sorting, no repeated full edge or port scans, and no dominator classifier.
- `withSpacedPositions`: O(N). The count check is O(1) and runs first. Then one `map`, and edges are reused by reference.
- The test file has an actual 500-node, 2,000-edge section with chain, star and dense-cycle shapes. Its read counter is a test-side Proxy, so there is no production instrumentation and no time thresholds.
