453: 
454: ### Submission markers and refusals (#244)
455: 
456: Initialization is session identity, not input acceptance. The engine persists exact pending
457: prompts and visit provenance before invoking either adapter; see [recovery](05-execution-engine.md#inference-submission-and-recovery).
458: The run event `harness.submission` says `submitted` with a marker on positive acceptance;
459: only failed or interrupted turns use the three failure classifications.
460: 
461: | Adapter            | Positive `submitted` marker                                                                                                                                                                                                  | What does not prove acceptance                                                                                     |
462: | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
463: | Claude stream-json | First validated non-error `assistant` record producing text, thinking or a tool call (`claude.assistant`); a validated successful `result` record proves consumption when no assistant content was emitted (`claude.result`) | `system/init`, the policy transcript item, commands/UI metadata, status, partial-message and rate-limit records    |
464: | Codex SDK          | `turn.started` (`codex.turn.started`), the input-accepted turn boundary after thread initialization                                                                                                                          | `thread.started`, `resumeThread`, the adapter's early known-ID announcement, configuration/deprecation error items |
465: 
466: Markers emit once per adapter turn. Claude emits `claude.result` as soon as a successful
467: result record passes session, model, usage and result-text validation, before waiting for CLI exit.
468: The engine records that acceptance with the visit and turn IDs immediately. Usage totals,
469: turn completion and the final result still wait for a verified process outcome; a later
470: transport failure cannot retract the observed acceptance or publish the buffered result.
471: A lost stream after init or after a marker is uncertain;
472: markers never claim provider-side durability beyond the observed protocol boundary. A process
473: kill before the marker is durably recorded remains uncertain too.
