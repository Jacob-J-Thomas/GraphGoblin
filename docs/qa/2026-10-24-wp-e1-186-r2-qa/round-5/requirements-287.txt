## Intent acceptance

## Source issue coverage

#117, #186, #187, #190, #259, #268

## Specific owner feedback

- The owner explicitly confirms which selected history is delete-ready. Failure/success does not change deletion eligibility. Automatic pressure cleanup cannot infer consent from age, completion, or a broad future-history preference. If an explicit loop-delete flow removes associated history, its confirmation must clearly cover that history; do not silently use loop deletion to bypass consent.
- Measure sustained loops early because they are central to the POC. Bounded extraction follows actual responsibility/performance evidence; do not assume file size proves a performance problem or mandate a general RunManager rewrite.

## Approved review refinements

Measure realistic sustained work early in #190 without waiting for unlimited mode. Optimize measured costs, page/reconnect history losslessly and bound temporary buffers/rendering. Cleanup uses explicit eligibility plus reference/ownership checks; pressure never broadens deletion authority.
