  - The lock passed from A4 (api coverage) directly to B4 (editor-forms Edge QA) without the job acquiring it. Its record is `raw/runs/C1-coverage-head.log`.

- **The real installed Codex CLI was not run.** It is the owner CLI and is excluded.
  - The unconfirmed path was exercised with a fake CLI whose detached grandchild keeps the pipes.
  - Q06 shows that a non-detached grandchild with inherited stdio is ended with its parent on Windows. The mechanism (probably libuv's kill-on-close job object) and the real native binary's behaviour are not established.
- **The enabled #511 lifecycle was covered only by the maintained `live-collection.test.ts` (F1).** I did not run my own `LIVE=1` run, so that no real CLI could be reached by mistake.
- Only Windows was exercised. macOS and Linux were not.
- Failure frequency with real CLIs is not established. An unconfirmed check leaves its probe directory permanently (Q05). This is by contract (see `REVIEW.md`, observation 1).
