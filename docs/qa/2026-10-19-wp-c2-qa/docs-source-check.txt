A paused run acknowledges its timer without waking; resume and recovery re-arm its recorded deadline. Timers for unreadable run history are also acknowledged, leaving that history for diagnostics and explicit deletion rather than logging on every poll. This prevents deferred rows from starving other overdue timers in a full batch. The original deadline stays in the wait spec.
